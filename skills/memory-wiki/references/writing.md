# Writing profile

## Seed requirements

Elicit or preserve:

- intended reader;
- desired change in that reader;
- the author's core claim;
- personal experience and opinions that must remain authoritative;
- tentative structure;
- must-use and must-avoid material;
- target form, length, and publication constraints.

Do not make the seed artificially polished. Its job is to preserve intent.

## Guideline

Write `guideline.md` for reasoning, not style. Include:

1. reader and promised transformation;
2. thesis and non-goals;
3. ordered narrative or argument beats;
4. claim-and-evidence map;
5. personal material and its intended placement;
6. counterarguments and tensions;
7. examples, transitions, and ending;
8. voice constraints and prohibited habits;
9. unresolved facts and explicit editorial choices.

Every factual claim must retain provenance. Label agent-proposed narrative choices as proposals rather than facts.

## Reflection

Check mechanically answerable questions:

- Does each central claim have adequate evidence?
- Does the structure deliver the promised reader change?
- Are disagreements represented rather than averaged away?
- Does every personal assertion come from the human?
- Are required sections, examples, and constraints present?
- Are uncertain or time-sensitive claims visibly unresolved?

## Decision review

Resolve editorial choices from the seed, evidence, and prior user direction when possible. Ask only questions whose answers would materially change the piece and cannot be inferred safely. Focus on:

- what the author truly believes;
- which transformation or story is emotionally honest;
- what is distinctive rather than generic;
- which tradeoffs the article should take a position on;
- where the argument overclaims or avoids tension.

Ask in small batches. Incorporate answers into the guideline and record rejected alternatives in `critique.md`.

## Rendering

Render only after the guideline passes critique and lint and the project reaches `ready`. Treat the guideline as the reasoning source of truth and avoid reopening settled research. Use a separate prose pass optimized for clarity, rhythm, specificity, and the selected voice. When model controls exist, prefer lower reasoning for this pass.

After rendering:

- preserve a private claim-to-source report;
- flag sentences whose certainty exceeds their evidence;
- require human editing;
- record material deviations from the guideline.
